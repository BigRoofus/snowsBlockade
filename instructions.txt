Introducing Blockade — the plugin that keeps your sound yours. Snap it onto any track and it works in total silence, sculpting inaudible high-frequency detail and phase-shifted peaks that humans never notice and machines can't ignore. One knob. Zero compromise. Protect your sound at the source.
